Published figures (about 21 seats, 3 cabins, 2 meeting rooms seating about 4 people each, and a day pass or monthly seat rate) describe the centre. Availability on a given day is confirmed at booking. We may move you to a comparable seat if a room is under repair or needed for safety.

  • A day pass is for one person, one operating day, and one seat in the open hall.
  • A monthly seat is a dedicated or hot desk for the agreed calendar period, fees in advance.
  • A cabin or full-floor arrangement follows the written commercial terms we send you.
  • A meeting room is for the booked hours only. Overrun time is charged if the room is still occupied.

2. Fees, tax, and payment

Unless a written quote says otherwise, the asking rates are ₹6,000 per seat per month and ₹300 for a day pass. GST applies where the law requires it and is extra unless the quote says the price is inclusive. Invoices are payable in advance by the mode we accept (bank transfer, UPI, or another method we name). If you deduct tax at source under the Income-tax Act, 1961, you will deposit it on time and share the certificate. A security deposit, if the quote asks for one, is held against damage, keys, unpaid fees, and extra services. We return the balance after you vacate and we inspect the seat, usually within 15 business days.

3. Cancellation and notice

  • Day pass. Cancel by 6:00 pm IST the day before the visit and we refund the pass fee, less any payment-gateway charge. A same-day cancellation is not refunded.
  • Meeting room. Cancel at least one full day before the booking and we refund half the room fee. Later cancellations are not refunded. If we cancel because the room is unavailable, we refund the room fee in full or offer another slot.
  • Monthly seat or cabin. Either side may end the arrangement by written notice of 30 days, unless the signed agreement sets a fixed term. Fees for the notice period stay payable. We do not refund a month that has already started because you stop coming.
  • Refunds we owe are started within 10 business days. Your bank may take longer to credit them.

We may end access immediately, without a refund of the unused period, if you break these terms, create a safety risk, or fail to complete identity checks we reasonably ask for.
